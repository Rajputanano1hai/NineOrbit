Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much budget do I need for Google Ads?+
It depends on your industry, competition and goals — we'll review your market and recommend a realistic starting budget rather than a one-size-fits-all number.
Can you guarantee leads from Google Ads?+
No — ad performance depends on market conditions, competition and your offer. We focus on sound campaign structure, targeting and tracking to give your budget the best realistic chance.
Do I need a new website for Google Ads to work?+
Not always, but your landing page needs to load quickly and match what the ad promises. We can review your current pages or build dedicated landing pages if needed.
